How your signup is used

Joining testing shares your email address with the developer, Gauravjeet Singh, to coordinate invitations and follow-up about testing Punjabi Watch. Use the email address associated with Google Play.

The form sends your submission directly to Formspree. Formspree stores submissions in the developer’s account and can deliver them to the configured email inbox. Its handling of submission and connection information is described in Formspree’s privacy policy.

To withdraw your interest or ask for removal of your signup, email gaurav.ishwerdas@gmail.com.

This signup is separate from the offline Wear OS watch face. Its existing watch-face privacy policy remains available at the same address.
