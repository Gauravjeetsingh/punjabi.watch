01 / Language

Every minute, in Gurmukhi.

From ਸਵਾ ਦਸ to ਡੇਢ ਵੱਜੇ and ਢਾਈ ਵੱਜੇ. The same time expressions as the live clock, with a heading for the time of day.

02 / Always-on

Still there at a glance.

In always-on mode, the words remain visible and update each minute, with dimmer text on black. Enable always-on display in your watch settings.

03 / Compatibility

Made for Wear OS.

A standalone watch face for Wear OS 4 and newer. No companion phone app is required. Bedtime and battery-saving modes may turn the display off.
