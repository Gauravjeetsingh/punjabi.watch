Privacy Policy

Punjabi Watch — Wear OS watch face

Effective date and last updated: October 13, 2026
Developer: Gauravjeet Singh

The Punjabi Watch watch face works offline and does not collect, store, or share personal data.

What this policy covers

This policy explains how Gauravjeet Singh handles information in connection with Punjabi Watch, the Wear OS watch face distributed under the package name com.raga.punjabiwatchface, and support requests about it.

It covers the current standalone watch face. A separate phone app, home-screen widget, or future service is not covered by this policy unless explicitly added in an update.

Information used by the watch face

Punjabi Watch uses the watch’s local hour and minute to display the time and a time-of-day heading in Punjabi. Wear OS renders this information locally on your watch. The watch face does not transmit it to the developer or to a server.

  • No account, registration, or sign-in is required.
  • The watch face has no network permission and makes no internet requests.
  • It does not collect location, health or fitness data, contacts, device identifiers, or usage history.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party data-collection SDKs.

The watch’s own time and time-zone settings determine the displayed time. Punjabi Watch does not request location access to determine your time zone.

Sharing and third-party services

No personal data is collected or shared by the watch face, and the developer does not sell personal data.

Google Play, Wear OS, and your device manufacturer may independently process information to provide their services, including installation, updates, and system diagnostics. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies and your device settings. See Google’s Privacy Policy for Google’s services.

If you contact us

If you email 1322panda@gmail.com, we receive your email address and any name, message, or attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request, troubleshoot issues, and provide support. This information is supplied by you through email; it is not collected automatically by the watch face.

Support messages are processed and stored by our email provider, Google/Gmail. We do not use support correspondence for advertising or sell it. We share it only as needed to provide the email service or when required by law.

Retention, deletion, and security

The watch face creates no personal-data records for the developer to retain or delete, and there is no Punjabi Watch account to close. You can remove the watch face through your device’s settings.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as needed to handle your request and related follow-up, or to meet applicable legal obligations. You can request access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent by contacting the email address below. Some information may need to be retained where required by law.

We restrict access to support correspondence to what is needed to handle your request and use the email provider’s account and transmission security protections. Please do not send passwords, payment information, or other unnecessary sensitive information in support messages.

Children’s privacy

Punjabi Watch is intended for users aged 13 and older. The watch face does not collect personal information from users of any age. If you believe a child has sent personal information through a support message, contact us so we can address it and delete that information where appropriate.

Visiting this page

This privacy page contains no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, cookies, or external font requests. When you visit it, the website hosting provider may process ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, browser information, and request time, to deliver and secure the website. This is separate from using the offline watch face.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the watch face or our information-handling practices change. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated date. If a future feature changes how personal data is handled, we will update the relevant disclosures and obtain consent where required before that processing begins.
